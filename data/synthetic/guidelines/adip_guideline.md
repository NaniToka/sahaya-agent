# Assistance to Disabled Persons for Purchase/Fitting of Aids/Appliances (ADIP) Scheme

**Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities (DEPwD)**
**Version: 3.0**
**Date: 01-Apr-2021**

## Section 1: Objective
The main objective of the ADIP Scheme is to assist the needy disabled persons in procuring durable, sophisticated and scientifically manufactured, modern, standard aids and appliances that can promote their physical, social and psychological rehabilitation, by reducing the effects of disabilities and enhancing their economic potential.

## Section 2: Eligibility Criteria
To receive an assistive device under this scheme:
1. **Clause 2.1**: Must be an Indian citizen of any age.
2. **Clause 2.2**: Must possess a 40% [TO VERIFY] disability certificate.
3. **Clause 2.3**: Monthly income from all sources must not exceed Rs. [TO VERIFY] 22,500.
4. **Clause 2.4**: Must not have received assistance during the last [TO VERIFY] 3 years for the same purpose from any source (except for children below 12 years, for whom the limit is [TO VERIFY] 1 year).
5. **Clause 2.5 (Ambiguous)**: The prescribed assistive device must be deemed absolutely essential for the daily functioning of the individual by the recommending authority.

## Section 3: Benefits
- If monthly income is up to Rs. [TO VERIFY] 15,000: Full cost of the aid/appliance is provided (up to Rs. [TO VERIFY] 15,000).
- If monthly income is Rs. [TO VERIFY] 15,001 to Rs. [TO VERIFY] 22,500: 50% of the cost of the aid/appliance is provided.
- Devices include tricycles, wheelchairs, hearing aids, Braille slates, and motor-driven vehicles (with specific conditions).

## Section 4: Required Documents
1. Disability Certificate.
2. Income Certificate issued by Revenue Authority / BPL card.
3. Aadhaar Card.
4. Undertaking that the applicant has not received the same aid in the past [TO VERIFY] 3 years.

## Section 5: How to Apply
Applications can be made at designated camps organized by ALIMCO or through National Institutes.
- **Portal**: ADIP portal or during scheduled assessment camps.
