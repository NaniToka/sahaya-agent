# Pre-Matric and Post-Matric Scholarships for Students with Disabilities

**Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities (DEPwD)**
**Version: 1.2**
**Date: 20-May-2022**

## Section 1: Objective
To support students with disabilities in pursuing their education from Class 9 onwards by providing financial assistance.

## Section 2: Eligibility Criteria
1. **Clause 2.1**: The student must be an Indian national.
2. **Clause 2.2**: The student must have a benchmark disability of [TO VERIFY] 40% or more.
3. **Clause 2.3**: For Pre-Matric (Class 9 & 10), the student's parents'/guardians' annual income must not exceed Rs. [TO VERIFY] 2,50,000.
4. **Clause 2.4**: For Post-Matric (Class 11 to Post-Graduation), the student's parents'/guardians' annual income must not exceed Rs. [TO VERIFY] 2,50,000.
5. **Clause 2.5 (Ambiguous)**: The student must maintain satisfactory academic progress and good conduct, as certified by the head of the institution.

## Section 3: Benefits
- Maintenance Allowance: Rs. [TO VERIFY] 500 - 1200 per month depending on the level of study and hosteler/day scholar status.
- Book Allowance: Rs. [TO VERIFY] 1500 per annum.
- Disability Allowance: Additional allowances for specific disabilities (e.g., Reader Allowance for visually impaired).

## Section 4: Required Documents
1. UDID Card or Disability Certificate.
2. Income Certificate of parents.
3. Previous year's mark sheet.
4. Aadhaar linked bank account details.
5. Fee receipt from the institution.

## Section 5: How to Apply
All applications must be routed through the National Scholarship Portal (NSP).
- **Portal**: scholarships.gov.in
- The application must be verified by the school/college principal.
