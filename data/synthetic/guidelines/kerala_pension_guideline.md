# Indira Gandhi National Disability Pension Scheme (IGNDPS) - Kerala Implementation

**Department of Social Justice, Government of Kerala**
**Version: 1.4**
**Date: 10-Aug-2022**

## Section 1: Objective
To provide financial assistance to Persons with Severe and Multiple Disabilities living below the poverty line.

## Section 2: Eligibility Criteria
1. **Clause 2.1**: The applicant must be a permanent resident of Kerala.
2. **Clause 2.2**: The applicant must be in the age group of [TO VERIFY] 18 to [TO VERIFY] 79 years.
3. **Clause 2.3**: The applicant must belong to a Below Poverty Line (BPL) household.
4. **Clause 2.4**: The applicant must have a severe disability of at least [TO VERIFY] 80% or multiple disabilities.
5. **Clause 2.5 (Ambiguous)**: The applicant must not be receiving any other substantial financial assistance from state or central government, as determined by the welfare officer.

## Section 3: Benefits
Eligible beneficiaries will receive a monthly pension amount.
- **Central Contribution**: Rs. [TO VERIFY] 300 per month.
- **State Contribution**: Rs. [TO VERIFY] 1300 per month.
- **Total Pension**: Rs. [TO VERIFY] 1600 per month, disbursed directly to the beneficiary's bank account.

## Section 4: Required Documents
1. BPL Ration Card copy.
2. Medical Certificate showing minimum [TO VERIFY] 80% disability.
3. Aadhaar Card.
4. Bank Account Passbook copy (linked with Aadhaar).
5. Age proof (Voter ID, Aadhaar, or Birth Certificate).

## Section 5: How to Apply
Applications should be submitted at the local self-government institution level.
- **Office**: Submit to the Secretary of the respective Grama Panchayat, Municipality, or Municipal Corporation.
- Online tracking is available via the Sevana Pension portal.
