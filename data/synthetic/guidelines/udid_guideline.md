# Guidelines for the Issuance of Unique Disability Identity (UDID) Card

**Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities (DEPwD)**
**Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment, Government of India**
**Version: 2.1**
**Date: 15-Jan-2023**

## Section 1: Objective
The objective of this scheme is to build a holistic end-to-end integrated system for Issuance of Universal ID & Disability Certificates for Person with Disabilities with their identification and disability details.

## Section 2: Eligibility Criteria
To be eligible for a UDID card, the applicant must satisfy the following criteria:
1. **Clause 2.1**: The applicant must be a citizen of India.
2. **Clause 2.2**: The applicant must have a benchmark disability of at least [TO VERIFY] 40% as certified by a competent medical authority. 
3. **Clause 2.3**: For disabilities below [TO VERIFY] 40%, a Disability Certificate may be issued, but they will not be considered "Persons with Benchmark Disability".
4. **Clause 2.4 (Ambiguous)**: The severity of the disability must be verified and confirmed as genuine by the competent medical authority based on their clinical assessment.

## Section 3: Benefits
The UDID card brings a host of benefits to the Persons with Disabilities:
- No need to make multiple copies of documents, maintain, and carry multiple documents. 
- The card will be the single document of identification, verification of the disabled for availing various benefits in future.
- The card captures tracking of the physical and financial progress of beneficiaries at all levels of hierarchy of implementation.

## Section 4: Required Documents
Applicants need to provide the following documents:
1. Recent Passport size Photo.
2. Aadhaar Card or any other valid Identity Proof.
3. Address Proof (if Aadhaar is not available or has a different address).
4. Previous Disability Certificate (if applying for renewal or conversion).

## Section 5: How to Apply
Applications must be submitted exclusively through the centralized online portal:
- **Portal**: Swavlamban Card Portal (www.swavlambancard.gov.in)
- Offline paper forms are no longer accepted unless authorized by the District Magistrate.
